# Agent: Bootstrap CRM System

**Purpose:** Contact & sales management offline-first

**Modules:**
- Contacts (names, phones, emails)
- Companies (organization data)
- Deals (sales pipeline)
- Interactions (calls, emails, meetings)
- Reports (pipeline, forecasting)

**Skills:**
1. docker-infrastructure
2. postgresql-multi-tenant
3. sync-engine-setup (bidirectional contact sync)
4. jwt-offline-auth
5. conflict-resolution (contact dedup)
6. data-integrity
7. celery-async-jobs (email integration)
8. postgresql-backup

**Time:** 2 weeks MVP

**Key:** Lightweight local contacts (mobile-friendly) + central sync
